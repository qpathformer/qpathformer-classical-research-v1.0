# Strategic retention or use review record

Template only; not an approval, licence or contract. It records an existing agreement and review; completion does not itself create the agreement. It concerns contributed private material proposed for Strategic retention or a new Strategic use. Completed records and any sensitive asset details remain in appropriately restricted private storage.

- Asset identities, parent records, contribution source and applicable research cycle:
- Existing audience, rights, public disclosures and permissions already granted:
- Submitter, actual contributor/applicable rights holder(s) where different, and authority evidence:
- Authorised Q-Pathformer representative and authority evidence:
- Proposed Strategic retention/use, purpose, audience and repository/storage destination:
- Applicable written agreement or permission reference, permitted uses, restrictions, attribution and duration, disclosure terms and any expiry:
- Each required party's recorded agreement and date; additional upstream permissions if needed:
- Rights, reproducibility, security and disclosure reviews, including unresolved items:
- Decision, reason, release eligibility, review date and owner:
- Reviewed file identities, accepted merge/asset record and later change history:

Confirm that the decision does not imply copyright assignment, exclusivity, new permissions or withdrawal of already-public rights beyond the actual applicable agreement. If required consent is absent, defer the proposed new Strategic use. Record review access separately from permission to execute, train, publish or distribute.

## Relationship to the completed studies

The document supplies historical context for the three-state concept: outer sphere (State 1), inner cube (State 2), and inner Q-sphere (State 3). The newer graphic's readable descriptions draw on later proposal work. The classical studies evaluate selected circuits, routers, readouts, sampling and allocation mechanisms. They do not demonstrate the complete conceptual architecture, a physical-QPU advantage, or a quantum language model.

# Research editions and participation

## Where to contribute now

Research v1.0 accepts public questions, corrections, reproduction reports and research proposals through [issues](https://github.com/qpathformer/qpathformer-classical-research-v1.0/issues/new) and permitted [pull requests](https://github.com/qpathformer/qpathformer-classical-research-v1.0/pulls). Follow the [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md); implementation patches require its confirmed incoming-rights route.

For private-intake enquiries, contact Anthony Siljeg at [asiljeg@alnotrea.com](mailto:asiljeg@alnotrea.com) with a non-sensitive description. Agree the transfer route and review scope before sending restricted payloads. Contact alone does not provide a secure transfer service, membership or a licence grant. Completed access, consent and restricted asset records belong in appropriate private storage.

## How the next research cycle works

1. Contribute permitted findings to the public research edition.
2. Develop the next defined research cycle privately with approved participants in a Private Research Lab.
3. Review evidence, reproducibility, rights, safety and disclosure.
4. Publish the exact owner-approved selection.
5. Continue, defer or retain other work under its applicable terms, then review participation for the next cycle.

Research v1.0 is the current public foundation. Research v2.0 is proposed to begin privately; a selected v2.0 edition may later become public. Research v3.0 and later editions follow the same process. The private Lab and Strategic workspace remain proposed until separately provisioned. No dates, complete disclosure or future membership are promised.

The [governance audience table](../GOVERNANCE.md#audiences) defines Public, Enterprise/Lab and Strategic. These participation/access categories are independent of the architecture's States 1, 2 and 3. Research editions, Enterprise Standards releases and Access-PoD document Versions A/B/C are separate schemes.

## Separate internal development

Strategic development is optional and separately authorised. It may run alongside a Lab cycle; a Lab study does not have to pass through Strategic development before public-release review.

Strategic materials are restricted to specifically authorised internal Q-Pathformer developers. Organisation owners retain approval and administrative authority. An internal-development appointment and explicit access grant are separate from Lab admission.

“Internal” identifies a project role, not employment status or organisation-owner privileges. Proposed work may include model development, safety protocols and cryptographic derivation/re-derivation research. Each technical mechanism requires its own specification and validation; this scope does not assert an implemented or verified capability.

Transfers into or out of the Strategic audience require the applicable [asset review](ASSET_REVIEW.md), rights and disclosure decisions. The Strategic workspace stays separate from the Private Research Lab and public Enterprise Standards projects.

## Participation and continued access

At least two distinct, substantive qualifying merged public contributions establish eligibility for owner review, not admission. The [canonical eligibility rule](../GOVERNANCE.md#eligibility-for-lab-access) defines qualifying work, owner assessment and recorded access scope. Useful classical work and negative findings can qualify; buying QPU time is not required.

Access is reassessed for each cycle. Record which prior qualifying contributions were considered; neither membership carryover nor a requirement for two newly earned merges is automatic. Lab admission grants no Strategic access, authorship or authority to publish.

## Publication and retained work

Publish a curated public selection from a reviewed snapshot while preserving withheld material and private development history. The [release policy](RELEASE_POLICY.md) governs the exact snapshot and asset decisions. Do not expose a working private repository's entire history merely to publish its selected findings.

For contributed private material proposed for Strategic retention or a new Strategic use, record an asset-specific agreement with the contributor or applicable rights holder and the authorised project representative. Identify the permitted purpose, audience, duration and disclosure terms, together with any additional permissions required. Existing public permissions remain unchanged.

The submitter may differ from the rights holder; identify the person or entity authorised to grant the relevant rights. Classification, a merge or Lab membership does not imply assignment, exclusivity, confidentiality, an incoming licence or an additional restriction on already licensed public content. If the necessary agreement is missing, defer the proposed new use; existing applicable rights and obligations continue to govern existing material.

The [retention review template](../templates/STRATEGIC_RETENTION_REVIEW.md) records an agreement and review. Completing it does not itself create the agreement.

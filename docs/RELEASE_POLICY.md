# Research release policy

Repository policy adopted on 31 October 2026. A release is an owner-approved snapshot of identified papers and supporting materials. The scientific paper, research approach and repository rules are distinct documents. The [research approach](RESEARCH_APPROACH.md) must be linked in the release notes and included under release docs; it is excluded from the main paper.

## Consolidated v1 release

The main findings paper is Q-Pathformer Routed States: Classical Models and Quantum Circuit Simulation, supplied as Word and PDF. The separate Three-State Architecture and Experimental Specification is its companion. The papers carry research edition v1.0 without editorial draft labels; covers are intentionally clean. Scientific proposal/validation limits remain explicit. The Papers ZIP contains these two papers, ten unchanged supporting study reports and the unchanged QPATH-ARCH-001 original from Git. Original source bytes and exact source-backed changes remain in the private owner record.

The Consolidated ZIP contains the Papers ZIP/seal pair and the identified repository snapshot. Its separate Research Approach is at repository/docs/RESEARCH_APPROACH.md; governance, contribution guidance, reproducibility requirements and the publication notice remain repository documents. Historical reports stay individually identified. Do not treat an assembled archive as a new experiment or an empirical endorsement of the architecture proposal.

The current [research-v1.0-20261031 release](https://github.com/qpathformer/qpathformer-classical-research-v1.0/releases/tag/research-v1.0-20261031) was published on 31 October 2026 and is marked Latest. The owner subsequently approved public repository access on the same date. Earlier packaging entries, including research-v1.0, were removed at the owner's request. Their original Git tags and verified private recovery archives are retained.

The published release body, five offered assets and source commit remain unchanged during later README/navigation updates. The current main branch may contain later guidance and status corrections, identified in [edition status](EDITION_STATUS.md). A navigation update does not require rebuilding a sealed release. Any later packaging edition must have an unambiguous identity and reviewed source commit; do not force-move historical tags.

The repository is public and research-v1.0-20261031 remains Latest. Live repository rules and the organisation profile are maintained separately from the sealed publication; see [governance](../GOVERNANCE.md).

## Publication checks

Confirm final paper identity and title; complete substantive review; preserve supported claims and limitations. The standalone research approach original prose has its own adopted CC BY 4.0 scope. The owner has adopted the current controlled original paper, cover and helper selection under CC BY 4.0; its exact scope and exclusions are recorded in [the current content scope](../RESEARCH_V1_CONTENT_LICENSE_SCOPE.json). Check retained and third-party notices separately. Existing report grants do not automatically license the new findings manuscript, architecture companion or all helper files.

Review the exact publication snapshot and every included asset. For an existing repository, examine all reachable Git history and branches, tags, releases, issues, PRs, workflow logs/artifacts, LFS objects and linked external storage. Replacing the current README or removing one release does not conceal earlier content. Preserve private records outside the public distribution. If a full public disclosure review cannot clear the existing history, prepare a clean public distribution while retaining a private canonical archive.

Verify manifests, checksums, links, licence notices, authorship and reading order. Verify public/private separation using a non-member account before sharing access. Review scientific claims and safety results separately from archive integrity. Publish only after the owner approves the exact hashes and destination.

## Later editions

Keep private v2.0 development and Strategic material separate from its eventual curated public edition. Owners decide what passes disclosure review and what stays private for v3.0 or later. Repeat contribution eligibility and owner approval for the next private cycle, rechecking participation scope rather than automatically carrying access forward. Retain the [publication notice](PUBLICATION_NOTICE.md) in each edition.

# Reading the consolidated research edition

The earlier research-v1.0 release is published within the private repository. This replacement retains research edition v1.0 and is prepared under the distinct tag research-v1.0-20261031 for the owner’s release check. The controlled-content licence and trademark policy have been adopted; public visibility remains a separate decision. Start with [Q-Pathformer Routed States](papers/QPathformer_Main_Paper_v1.0.pdf), the main findings paper on classical models and quantum circuit simulation. It reports v0.6, v0.9 and v0.9.5 as distinct experiments, including negative and inconclusive findings.

Read [Q-Pathformer Three-State Architecture and Experimental Specification](papers/QPathformer_Architecture_and_Experimental_Specification_v1.0.pdf) for the proposed system, protocols, evidence map and unresolved questions. It is the architecture companion; the full integrated architecture remains unvalidated.

The [original architecture PDF](architecture/QPATH-ARCH-001.pdf) is preserved unchanged. Its first page records an introduction on LinkedIn on 5 December 2024. The [provenance record](architecture/README.md) distinguishes that introduction from the retained PDF's March 2026 creation and later repository additions.

The Papers ZIP includes those three works and ten supporting reports. The [repository study index](../README.md) links the individual Study, Documentation and Code assets. The [Research Approach](RESEARCH_APPROACH.md), [contribution rules](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [publication notice](PUBLICATION_NOTICE.md) remain separate release documentation. The private v2.0 Lab programme is proposed future work; its membership rules are not part of either scientific paper.

The [collection cover](../assets/covers/QPathformer_Collection_Cover_v1.0.pdf) uses the overall Research Framework for Routed Learning title. Each new paper has its own title, cover, bibliography and metadata. Read [edition status](EDITION_STATUS.md) for remaining rights and publication decisions.

The [main-paper change note](MAIN_PAPER_CHANGE_NOTE.md) and [architecture-companion change map](ARCHITECTURE_COMPANION_CHANGE_NOTE.md) document the preserved scientific content and the editorial status and metadata updates in `research-v1.0-20261031`. The earlier `research-v1.0` files remain preserved in their historical release. This replacement has its own package and manuscript identities.

The nested geometry follows page 3 of QPATH-ARCH-001. The readable labels draw on the later classical architecture proposal, v0.3. They summarize responsibilities rather than quoting the original terminology. The concept's forward organization is State 3 → State 2 → State 1.

The colored rays are visual relationships, not measured Bloch vectors, physical qubits or numerical results. The completed studies examine selected circuits, routers, readouts, sampling and allocation; they do not validate a full quantum language model.

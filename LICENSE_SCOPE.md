The exact copied code/report scopes refer to files within their original payload trees in the companion packages, not implementation or report files in this documentation tree. The source documentation scope identifies its source editions; it is not a current-byte inventory for edited or moved files. The [current source map](SOURCE_MAP.json) identifies current files, source identities and changes, while the [documentation index](DOCUMENTATION_LICENSE_SCOPE.json) distinguishes retained/adapted source content from new additions.

Content already licensed through the report or documentation grant remains reusable where it also appears in another document or archive. This does not license all surrounding material. Excluding an architecture document does not remove permissions in expression actually licensed through selected prose or illustrations. Publisher hashes identify editions; permitted extracts, translations and adaptations need not retain original filenames, formats or digests.

The Platform license's commercial/production and distribution conditions apply to its identified code. They do not carry over to CC-licensed report or documentation content. The unchanged [CC BY 4.0 legal text](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt) governs the CC grants.

Anthony Siljeg remains the named licensor. Attribution: Founder, Alnotrea Labs; company affiliation, Alnotrea Pty Ltd; project, Q-Pathformer Initiative. These details do not transfer ownership. No blanket incoming-contribution agreement is created. Datasets, model artifacts, full archives and future additions require their own identified treatment.

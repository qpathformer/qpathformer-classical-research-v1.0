## Where the licensed material is supplied

The selected code and report are separate [study-v0.9.5 release downloads](https://github.com/qpathformer/qpathformer-classical-research/releases/tag/study-v0.9.5). Their scope records are copied into this Git tree to identify those downloads; the records do not imply that the implementation and report files are stored here. Preparation-state fields in those preserved records describe their original captured state; the release and publication receipts identify the uploaded assets.

“Public report edition” is the report's edition title. This staging repository and its downloads remain private. Access restrictions do not revoke the recorded report-content grant.

The code is **source available under Platform 1.0**. The unchanged license text governs attribution, commercial/production use, derivative distribution and governance conditions. No Apache, MIT, Platform 1.1 or claim of OSI open-source licensing is adopted here. The owner confirmed control of the listed implementation, inherited kernels, ETEL core and review tools; the scope preserves that decision.

The report has its own **CC BY 4.0** grant. Platform 1.0 conditions do not carry over to that report content. The report scope identifies the publisher's edition; permitted extracts, translations and adaptations need not keep its filename, format or hash. Already-licensed report content retains that grant when reproduced in another document; this does not license the whole containing document or previously omitted material. [CC BY 4.0 legal text](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt).

Datasets, weights, adapters, checkpoints, exported predictors, installed dependency/runtime bundles and complete experiment archives are not supplied or licensed as whole collections by these notices. Any later distribution needs its own identified scope and applicable terms.

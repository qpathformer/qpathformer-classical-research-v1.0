Geometric routing consistently outperformed matched-count hash routing. The global Trig121 control led the two harder clean tasks (98.47% checkerboard and 96.57% spiral macro-F1 versus 76.57% and 86.82% for the flexible circuit). Classical compilation preserved checked class predictions and accelerated single-input circuit calls 4.28–4.58 times. These historical measurements do not establish quantum advantage.

608 registered fits, including 512 confirmation endpoints; 20 primary paired population-level BCE contrasts. Small-sample interval assumptions, four synthetic families and host-specific timing limit generalization. Finite-shot sensitivity was historical local simulation, not physical hardware.

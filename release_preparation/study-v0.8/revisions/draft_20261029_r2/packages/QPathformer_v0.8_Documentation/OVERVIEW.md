# Study v0.8 Priority Training and Routed Models

Ten arms pair uniform and priority sampling with MultiK, MultiHash, TrigK, TrigHash and TrigG121 on fresh checkerboard, spiral, noisy annulus and rare-disk populations. Confirmation uses eight independent populations per task with two paired optimizer seeds.

Priority improved four of eight direct registered BCE comparisons, with the other four inconclusive; none established more than 0.01 BCE improvement across the entire interval. Priority did not close the routed circuit gap to the wider global baseline on checkerboard or spiral. Exact conversion preserved predictions (maximum probability difference below 2.62e-14) and improved local latency.

40 engineering, 80 development and 640 confirmation fits, plus four preselected clean retrains. All 20 primary comparisons are retained. Reproduction fits are not new independent populations. Three timing sessions varied; retained source reports preserve these limits. No physical quantum or new finite-shot campaign is claimed.

ETEL 0.2.0 schedules whole fits. The included execution adapter is study-specific; its logical slots are not physical workers or model capacity. Historical host observations are not minimum hardware requirements.

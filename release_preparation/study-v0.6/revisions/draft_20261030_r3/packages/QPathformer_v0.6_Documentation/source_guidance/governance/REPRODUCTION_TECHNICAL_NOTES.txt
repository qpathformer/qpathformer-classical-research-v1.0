1. Preserve the evidence package

Never run computations inside a delivered or sealed package. Verification is
read-only. Use an explicit fresh writable copy, retain all original metadata,
and give every derived computation a fresh output directory. Do not delete an
old result to make a guard pass. Failed partial outputs must be preserved.

The exact root manifest excludes only itself and two root verification metadata
files. It includes sources, datasets, every-round training evidence, checkpoints,
official predictions, independent replays, simulator and deployment outputs,
snapshot JSON, source/analysis locks, report sources and scientific reviews.
Virtual environments, bytecode, generated synthetic test trees, plot caches,
Word page-render intermediates and a duplicate working Word file are excluded
at copy time. Their durable sources, logs and review evidence remain. Compact
quantum/preflight_evidence is retained; it is synthetic evidence, not trained-model
findings. Exact omissions are in governance/DELIVERY_COPY_RECORD.json.

PowerShell example: use a real final manifest SHA obtained from the parent
delivery record, not a guessed value. The names below are explicit examples.

  $qpathPackage = 'C:\Q2\Improvement_Study_v0.6'
  $qpathPython = 'C:\Q2\Implementation_v0.1\.venv\Scripts\python.exe'
  $qpathManifestSha = '<exact delivered manifest SHA256>'
  & $qpathPython -B (Join-Path $qpathPackage 'governance\verify_delivery_independent.py') --package-root $qpathPackage --expected-manifest-sha256 $qpathManifestSha --output 'C:\Anthony Siljeg\v06_verify_fresh.json'

The second verifier uses PowerShell 7 (System.Text.Json), independently of Python:

  & pwsh -NoProfile -File (Join-Path $qpathPackage 'governance\Verify_Delivery.ps1') -PackageRoot $qpathPackage

Both verification tools read payloads only. The Python --output must be absent
and outside the inspected package. Do not redirect a verifier log into the package.

To prepare a new copy, first run without --copy for a preflight, then explicitly
request --copy. Destination and receipt must be absent; neither may be inside
the source, and the receipt must also be outside the destination.

  & $qpathPython -B (Join-Path $qpathPackage 'governance\prepare_reproduction_copy.py') --package-root $qpathPackage --destination 'C:\Anthony Siljeg\QPath_v06_reproduction_001' --expected-manifest-sha256 $qpathManifestSha --receipt 'C:\Anthony Siljeg\v06_reproduction_copy_001.json' --copy
  $qpathCopy = 'C:\Anthony Siljeg\QPath_v06_reproduction_001'

The helper fully verifies the source and copied inventory before returning PASS.
After new derived outputs are added, the writable copy intentionally no longer
has the initial manifest inventory. The original delivered package remains intact.

3. Retained data and original provenance

Twelve independent populations cover engineering/development/confirmation for
checkerboard, spiral, noisy annulus and rare-positive disks. All populations have
8,000 records split4,800/1,600/1,600 with observed-label stratification preserving
prevalence. Training-only five-kmeans policies are frozen. Models consume raw2D
coordinates and applicable fixed routes. clean_y and label_flip are audit fields;
they are never model/router fitting or checkpoint-selection inputs.
Annulus flips are independent Bernoulli0.10, not forced to exactly10percent.

The package contains all36 NPZ files and12 router policies needed for retained
model/evaluation replay. DATA_REGISTRATION and DATA_SEAL bind formulas/seeds,
historical exclusions, sources, bytes and exact-coordinate novelty. Full original
generation also read65 historical NPZ sources outside this package. Recreating
that exclusion audit requires those exact historical inputs and reviewed path
mapping; no clean-room regeneration claim is made. The delivered saved data are
sufficient for the registered model, scoring and independent replay interfaces.

Registered absolute paths are provenance. The official evaluator and independent
replay validate those identities against original_working_root and remap to the
current package root; they do not rewrite the old registrations. The complete
copied-package synthetic preflight actively blocked original-tree access. Do not
globally replace paths inside sealed metadata to make another program run.

5. Derived deployment and simulator reproduction

The deployment producer has its own explicit new output argument and requires
root release plus completed official scoring. It compiles the same selected
Scalar/Multi logits into Trig25 coefficients without fitting or reselecting
checkpoints. It also uses direct fast trigonometric control representations.
The independent Laurent oracle checks coefficient construction separately.
Run this only when training/audits/compilation/other benchmarks are quiescent:

  & $qpathPython -B -u 'quantum\deployment_benchmark.py' --output (Join-Path $qpathCopy 'quantum\deployment_reproduction_001')

The canonical deployment result is quantum/deployment_confirmation. It contains
coefficient artifacts, validation and released-test parity, off-grid/route-boundary
checks, load/compile measurements, raw timing trials, summaries and a full commit.
All120 original models plus40 compiled and40 fast-trig representations yield600
batch conditions. Two warmups and15 timed trials produce10,200 timing rows,
of which9,000 are timed. The trials are technical repeats nested within checkpoint,
not new training seeds. Hot raw-input inference excludes model loading and fitting;
router/model work is included. Load/compile/artifact bytes are separate. Wallclock
timings depend on host/software/background load, not physical quantum-device time.

For the local capsule, choose the separate simulator environment explicitly:

  $qpathSimulatorPython = 'C:\Anthony Siljeg\v06_simulator_replay_env\Scripts\python.exe'
  & $qpathSimulatorPython -B -u 'quantum\local_readiness.py' --output (Join-Path $qpathCopy 'quantum\readiness_reproduction_001')

The public capsule gate requires the original complete confirmation release;

checkpoints/1,280 fixture pairs cover Scalar and Multi on all four populations.
Scalar has one XX measurement setting; Multi uses XX and ZZ. Three distinct

correlations; budgets are per setting. Shot repeats are nested within each

The actual independent audits are retained under

6. Validation/resource summaries and new optimizer runs

analysis/learning_resources_v06.py reads committed selected training/validation
and resource evidence. Its CLI requires --stage A|B, --author-lock,
--independent-audit and --output, with a fresh output folder under analysis.
The root release and completed independent scoring replay are prerequisites.
It describes90/95/97percent validation milestones with nonreachers censored and
reached-only medians explicitly conditional. For observed noisy-annulus labels,
97percent is not a sensible expected generalization requirement. All training
runs completed200rounds/15,000updates; milestone counts do not establish savings.
Training-loop seconds include validation and within-loop evidence writes;
they are wall time, not CPU process time. Parallel worker seconds cannot be
summed and labelled project elapsed time. Reference inference timers collected
during training are separate from the isolated deployment benchmark.

The retained original launch commands are quantum/launch_stage.py --stage A
and --stage B, but they use fixed absent stage folders. Never invoke them in a
delivered copy containing those runs, or remove stage evidence to allow them.

An explicitly authorized optimizer reproduction may use study.py with a fresh

This uses the same historical train/validation rows. Record separate reproduction
authorization; do not merge its evidence into stage_A/stage_B, replace an arm,
reselect the original checkpoint or count it as a new cohort. Official mode
refuses reduced budgets. No automatic resume is supported. New research needs
a new identity, fresh preregistered populations/seeds, audits and release chain.

7. Interpretation and limits

These are four synthetic2D boundary families, not a real tabular or language
benchmark. Exact-coordinate novelty does not establish semantic novelty. Seed
and conditional record uncertainty address different sources of variability.
An algebraically equivalent classical coefficient representation is a deployment
of the same fitted model, not an additional model-quality result or evidence
of quantum speedup. Finite fixtures and tolerance checks are not an every-input
floating-point proof. Coefficient-L1 bounds refer to the represented polynomials
and exclude floating-point evaluation error.

The retained simulation snapshot is dated and approximate. Shot effects are
reported as sampling/distortion with nested repeats, not evidence that noise
helps generalization. No hardware readiness or broad quantum advantage claim
follows from successful local software checks.

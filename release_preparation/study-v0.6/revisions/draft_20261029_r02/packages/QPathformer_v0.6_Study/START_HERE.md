# Q Pathformer Study v0.6 Study

**Harder Tasks and Faster Inference**

Six registered models compare scalar and multiple-moment circuit readouts with routed and global classical controls on checkerboard, spiral, noisy-annulus and rare-positive-disk families. The richer readout improves the difficult clean-boundary tasks; the routed trigonometric control remains stronger there. Exact classical coefficient conversion accelerates the same trained circuits without changing their decisions; it is not another efficacy arm. The study completed 48 development and 120 confirmation endpoints, plus 24 engineering endpoints.

Study v0.6 and repository edition v1.0 are separate. This release edition contains the adopted, separately scoped report, prose and Python grants. Read [rights](RIGHTS.md) and [verification](VERIFY.md).

[Report PDF](reports/QPathformer_v0.6_Report.pdf) · [Word report](reports/QPathformer_v0.6_Report.docx) · [Edition changes](provenance/EDITION_CHANGES.md).

The results concern small two-dimensional synthetic classifiers on classical CPU hardware. Five paired seeds and one split per family do not establish population-wide or real-world generalization. Historical prior-version scores are context, not paired evidence of improvement. Local compilation, noise and shot results are simulator evidence, with no physical-QPU result or quantum-advantage claim. The full proposed language architecture was not benchmarked.

The full report is preserved with its 3 figures and 39 tables. Historical full-delivery paths refer to retained evidence, not files in this curated download.
